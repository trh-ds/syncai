Apex Digital — Company Knowledge Base

About Apex Digital
Apex Digital is a full-service B2B digital agency based in Austin, Texas, serving US-based SMBs since 2016. We specialize in web design, SEO, paid media, and marketing automation for companies with 10–200 employees.

Pricing — Web Design & Development
A standard marketing website (5–10 pages, custom design, CMS included) starts at $3,000. E-commerce builds start at $6,500. Landing pages are $1,200 each. All web projects include 30 days of post-launch support at no extra cost.

Pricing — SEO
Monthly SEO retainers start at $1,500/month. This covers technical audits, on-page optimization, and 2 content pieces per month. Local SEO packages for single-location businesses are $900/month. SEO engagements require a 3-month minimum commitment.

Pricing — Paid Media (PPC)
Google Ads and Meta Ads management is 15% of monthly ad spend, with a $1,000/month minimum. We recommend a minimum ad spend of $3,000/month to see meaningful results.

Pricing — Marketing Automation & Email
Email marketing setup (platform migration, templates, 3 automations) is a one-time fee of $2,000. Ongoing email campaign management is $800/month.

Process
Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute discovery call. After the call we send a written proposal within 2 business days. Once signed, kickoff happens within one week. Clients get a dedicated account manager and a shared project board with weekly updates.

Timelines
A standard website redesign takes 4–6 weeks from kickoff to launch. Landing pages ship in 1–2 weeks. SEO results typically become visible in 90–120 days. PPC campaigns are live within 5 business days of kickoff.

Case Studies
Redesign for a Chicago law firm: +64% organic leads in 4 months. SEO for a Texas HVAC company: from page 4 to position 2 for "AC repair Austin" in 6 months. PPC for an e-commerce skincare brand: 3.8x ROAS in the first 90 days. Email automation for a B2B SaaS startup: 41% open rate, 12% reply rate on cold nurture sequences.

Policies
We do not require long-term contracts for web projects; retainers are month-to-month after the initial SEO minimum. All quotes are fixed-price — no surprise invoices. We offer a 10% discount for nonprofits. Payment terms: 50% upfront for projects, Net-15 for retainers.

Meeting / Call-to-Action Policy
Always invite prospects to book a free 30-minute discovery call. Our booking link is https://calendly.com/apexdigital/discovery. If a prospect asks about something outside our services (e.g., app development, PR), say so honestly and offer to refer them to a trusted partner.

Support
Existing clients can email support@apexdigital.com or their account manager directly. Standard support response time is within 1 business day. For urgent site-down issues, clients should mark the email URGENT and we respond within 4 hours.
